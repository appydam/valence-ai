# NICHE 1: DTC E-commerce Brands ($2M–$20M revenue)

## Who to target
- Head of Growth, E-commerce Director, Founder/CEO (for smaller brands)
- Shopify or WooCommerce brands in: beauty/skincare, wellness/supplements, apparel, home goods, pet products
- Signs they're a good fit: running Google/Meta Ads, have a Klaviyo account, 3+ active competitors

## The hook (one sentence)
> "We watch your competitors 24/7. The moment one of them drops prices, your counter-campaign is already written and ready to send — before your team even sees the alert."

## Full cold email

**Subject:** [BrandName] — your competitors just changed prices

**Body:**
> Hi [Name],
>
> Quick question: how long does it take your team to notice when [Competitor1] runs a flash sale — and how long to actually respond?
>
> For most brands your size, it's 24–48 hours. By then, you've already lost the weekend.
>
> We built an AI agent squad that monitors your top competitors daily. The moment they drop prices or launch a promotion, the agents analyze your margins, draft a counter-campaign (email + SMS + social), and route it to your team for one-click approval — all within 60 minutes.
>
> Same system also handles flash sale launches end-to-end (inventory check, copy, scheduling, post-sale reporting) and demand forecasting tied to your marketing calendar so you never stock out before a campaign.
>
> Worth a 20-minute call? I can show you exactly how it works with your actual competitors.
>
> — Arpit Dhamija

## What to show in the demo (15 min)
1. Pull up one of their actual competitor stores live
2. Show Scout detecting a simulated price change on a tracked SKU
3. Show Ghost generating the full counter-campaign (email subject lines, SMS, Instagram caption) in under 2 minutes
4. Show Sentinel verifying margin before sending
5. Show the Slack notification their team would receive

**The close line:** "If we catch one competitor sale your team would have missed, and the counter-campaign prevents even $3k in lost revenue — the pilot has paid for itself 1.2× before month one ends."

## Pilot proposal (send on Day 4)
- Duration: 2 weeks
- What they get: competitor monitoring on top 5 competitors, 1 flash sale run end-to-end, daily inventory demand report
- Price: Business Standard ($2,499/mo) — offer first month at $1,500 for pilot participants
- What you need from them: Shopify read access, list of 5–10 competitor URLs, Klaviyo API key

## Objection handling

**"We already use Prisync / we monitor this manually"**
> "Prisync shows you the data — it can't respond to it. The 10 hours your team spends monitoring and reacting manually every week is what we eliminate. The counter-campaign is the hard part."

**"Our team can write our own campaigns"**
> "Absolutely — but can they do it in 60 minutes at 6am on a Saturday when a competitor drops prices? That's when flash sales happen. We're the 24/7 shift your team can't staff."

**"We're not sure about AI-generated content for our brand"**
> "Every piece of copy is reviewed and approved by your team before it goes out. The AI does the draft in brand voice — your team does the final check. You send nothing you haven't approved."

# NICHE 2: Digital Marketing Agencies (10–100 clients)

## Who to target
- Agency Founder, VP Client Services, Director of Account Management
- Performance marketing agencies (paid search, paid social, SEO, email)
- Signs they're a fit: managing $500k–$10M in monthly ad spend across clients, team of 5–50 people, clients on retainer

> "Your account managers spend 10–15 hours a week building reports. We eliminate that entirely — verified data, written narrative, ready to send, every Friday at 3pm."

**Body:**

Every Friday, your agents pull data from Google Ads, Meta, GA4, email, and e-commerce — verify every number against source APIs — write the performance narrative with actual insights ("your Meta creative hit frequency 6.2, that's why ROAS dropped — rotate new creative next week") — and deliver a formatted report ready for your AM to personalize and send in 20 minutes instead of building from scratch in 12 hours.
>
> Your AMs go from reporting to strategy. Your agency can take on more clients without hiring.
>
> Would you be open to a 20-minute call to see it live? I'll walk through it with your actual client structure.
>
> — Arpit Dhamija

## What to show in the demo (15 min)
1. Show the data pull: GA4 + Meta + Google Ads + Klaviyo all connecting simultaneously

>

>

1. Show Scout pulling closed-lost deals from a demo Salesforce instance

# THE PILOT CLOSE FRAMEWORK (works for all 3 niches)

## The 5-day close timeline

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **Day 1** | Send cold email with niche-specific hook. Subject line is the hook, not the product name. |
| **Day 2** | If no reply: LinkedIn connection request + 1-line message referencing the email |
| **Day 3** | Demo call (20 min). Show the demo with their actual data/competitors. End with: "Want me to send over a pilot proposal?" |
| **Day 4** | Send pilot proposal doc: 2-week scope, what they get, what you need access to, price |
| **Day 5–7** | Follow up once. If no reply, move to next lead. Don't chase. |

## What to say at the end of every demo
> "Here's what I'd propose: a 2-week pilot. I connect to [their specific tools], we run [the specific use case they cared most about in the demo], and you see real output on your real data. If it's not clearly worth the investment, I'll tell you before we talk about a longer contract. The goal of the pilot is to make the decision obvious."

## Pricing anchor for pilots
- **DTC E-commerce:** $1,500 pilot (first month discount from $2,499) + waive $1,500 onboarding fee
- **Marketing Agency:** $1,500 pilot (same) — position against "$1,500 = 1.5 hours of AMs's time per client per week"
- **RevOps:** $2,500 pilot (discount from $4,999) — position against "1/3 of one SDR's monthly salary"

## The risk-reversal close (use when they hesitate on price)
> "Here's what I can offer: if after 2 weeks we haven't clearly surfaced [measurable outcome specific to their niche], I'll refund the pilot cost and we part as friends. I'd rather earn the relationship than lock you in."

# OUTREACH CHANNELS (priority order)

1. **LinkedIn DM** — warm > cold email. Connect first, then message. Reference a specific post they made.
2. **Email** — use the scripts above. Keep it under 150 words.
3. **Intro through your network** — fastest close. Any connection to VC/investors → portfolio companies → RevOps/Marketing leads.
4. **Communities** — Pavilion (RevOps/Sales), Exit Five (B2B marketing), Indie Hackers (DTC founders), SaaS product communities.

## Finding the right contact
- **DTC:** LinkedIn search "Head of Growth [company]" or "E-commerce Director [company]"
- **Agency:** LinkedIn search "VP Client Services [agency]" or "[agency name] founder"
- **RevOps:** LinkedIn search "Head of RevOps [company]" or "VP Sales [company Series B]"

---

# WHAT NOT TO SAY

- Don't say "AI platform" — say "AI agent squad" or "AI workforce"
- Don't say "automate your workflows" — say "does the work for you"
- Don't lead with features — lead with the specific outcome they care about
- Don't show the full Mission Control UI on the first demo — it's too much. Show the one use case they care about, end-to-end.
- Don't say "29 use cases" — say "we started with [their specific use case] and can expand from there"
- Don't mention pricing until they ask or until you've shown a demo
