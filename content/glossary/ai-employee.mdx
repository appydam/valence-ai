An **AI employee** is an autonomous AI agent deployed as an organizational resource — a worker that receives business goals, executes multi-step tasks using tool integrations, and delivers results without requiring human instruction at each step. The term distinguishes autonomous AI agents (which work independently) from AI assistants (which respond to human questions) and from AI tools (which augment individual human tasks).

The "employee" framing captures the operational relationship: like a human employee, an AI employee is given responsibilities, access to necessary tools, quality expectations, and escalation paths. Unlike a human employee, the AI employee operates continuously, scales instantly, and doesn't require onboarding beyond integration setup and mission configuration.

## What AI Employees Do

AI employees handle workflows that are:
- **Multi-step:** Requiring planning, tool use, and sequential or parallel execution
- **Tool-integrated:** Using business systems (CRM, Slack, email, project management) to gather input and deliver output
- **Judgment-limited:** Applying defined logic and quality standards, escalating when genuinely ambiguous

Concrete examples:
- **Sales AI employee:** Researches inbound leads, enriches CRM records, drafts personalized first-touch emails
- **Marketing AI employee:** Monitors competitor activity, generates content briefs, drafts blog posts
- **Operations AI employee:** Generates weekly status reports, reconciles cross-system data, prepares board meeting materials

## AI Employee vs. AI Assistant

An AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude in chat mode) responds to questions and helps with tasks when asked. The human initiates every interaction, reviews output, and decides next steps.

An AI employee initiates workflows based on triggers (new lead in CRM, weekly schedule, incoming webhook), executes multi-step processes autonomously, and delivers results without the human coordinating each step.

AI assistants augment individual productivity; AI employees handle organizational workflows.

## AI Employee vs. AI Tool

AI tools (Grammarly, Copilot, Jasper) augment specific human tasks within a human's workflow. The human remains the workflow owner; AI provides assistance at specific steps.

AI employees own entire workflows. The human submits a goal; the AI employee handles execution; the human reviews the final output. The workflow runs through the AI, not through the human.

## Deploying AI Employees

Enterprise AI employee deployment involves:
1. **Role definition:** What workflows will this AI employee own? What tools will it access?
2. **Integration setup:** Connecting relevant business systems via OAuth or API key
3. **Mission brief templates:** Standard goal formats for each workflow type
4. **Quality review process:** How output is reviewed before delivery to stakeholders
5. **Escalation paths:** When and how the AI employee routes to a human
