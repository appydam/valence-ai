**AI task automation** is the use of AI agents to autonomously execute individual business tasks that previously required manual human effort. Unlike rule-based task automation (macros, scripts, simple triggers), AI task automation handles tasks that require judgment, context awareness, and the ability to work with unstructured information — such as researching a prospect, drafting a personalized email, reviewing a contract clause, or writing a code fix.

The core advancement of AI task automation over traditional task automation is **intelligence**: the AI understands what the task requires, gathers the needed context, makes decisions about how to proceed, and produces outputs that reflect situational awareness.

## Examples of AI Task Automation

**CRM enrichment:** An AI agent researches a company using web sources, LinkedIn, and news articles, then updates the CRM record with current employee count, funding stage, technology stack, and recent developments — tasks that would take a human 15-30 minutes per record.

**Email drafting:** An AI agent reads a conversation thread, understands the context and relationship, and drafts a reply that matches the appropriate tone, references relevant details, and advances the conversation goal.

**Code generation:** An AI agent reads a bug report or feature request, examines the relevant codebase, writes the fix or feature, creates a pull request, and adds appropriate documentation.

**Report generation:** An AI agent pulls data from analytics tools, CRM, and project management systems, synthesizes the information into a narrative report, and delivers it in the requested format.

**Quality review:** An AI agent reviews another agent's or human's work against defined quality criteria — checking for accuracy, completeness, tone, compliance, and other standards.

## AI Task Automation vs. Mission Automation

AI task automation handles individual tasks. **Mission automation** (or workflow-level automation) coordinates multiple tasks into a coherent multi-step workflow. For example:

- **Task automation:** Draft a follow-up email for a prospect.
- **Mission automation:** Research 50 churning accounts, identify the top reasons for churn, draft personalized win-back emails for each, update CRM records, and schedule follow-ups.

The most capable AI platforms handle both levels. Individual tasks are the building blocks; missions are the business outcomes.

## AI Task Automation at Scale

At scale, AI task automation requires:

**Integration breadth:** Agents need access to the tools where tasks happen — CRM, email, code repositories, project management, analytics, and more.

**Quality control:** Automated tasks must meet the same quality standards as human-performed tasks. This requires quality gates, review agents, or human-in-the-loop approval.

**Context persistence:** Agents performing repeated tasks for the same organization should remember past context — customer preferences, brand voice, recurring patterns — to improve quality over time.

**Orchestration:** When many tasks need to happen in coordination, an orchestration layer decomposes goals, routes tasks to the right agents, and assembles the results.
