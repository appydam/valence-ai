## Product Hunt Listing (Draft)

**Name:** AgentHost Mini

**Tagline:** Right-click any company → 2-minute AI research brief

**Description:**
AgentHost Mini turns any company name into a research brief in 2 minutes.

**How it works:**
1. Right-click on any company name (LinkedIn, Twitter, news sites, anywhere)
2. Select "Research with AgentHost Mini"
3. Get instant company overview, recent news, funding, tech stack, and pain points

**Who it's for:**
- Sales professionals doing outbound prospecting
- VCs researching portfolio companies
- Business development teams evaluating partnerships
- Founders tracking competitors

**Why we built it:**
Every sales professional does the same research: Google the company, check their website, find their tech stack, read recent news, identify decision makers. This takes 10-15 minutes per company.

We automated it. Now it takes 2 minutes.

**Tech:**
- Chrome extension (Manifest V3)
- AI-powered research (OpenClaw agents)
- Free tier: 3 briefs per day
- Pro tier: Unlimited briefs ($15/mo)
